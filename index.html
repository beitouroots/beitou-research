📊 Jobs Week

Private Payrolls Grow Just 38,000 in August, the Slowest Pace Since January

  • The tea: US companies added 38,000 jobs in August, per ADP, down from an upwardly revised 46,000 in July and below the Dow Jones consensus estimate of 47,000, CNBC reported, the smallest gain since January. 📉
  • Where it came from: education and health services led with 45,000 adds and leisure and hospitality added 16,000, but manufacturing lost 17,000 and professional and business services shed 16,000. 🧮
  • Why it collides with Story 2: a cooling labor market usually argues for Fed patience, but with oil spiking and Warsh already leaning hawkish on inflation, today's soft ADP print puts Friday's official jobs report squarely in the driver's seat for the September 15-16 meeting. 🗳️
  • Tonight's test: Broadcom, Snowflake, and Hewlett Packard Enterprise all report after today's close, the next read on whether AI capex can keep carrying the market through this squeeze. 👀
