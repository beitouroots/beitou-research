Gold, Bitcoin and Stocks Are All Ripping at Once, That's the Debasement Trade Talking

  • The big idea: gold is up more than 1% and holding near $4,580 an ounce, Bitcoin just had its best week since March above $78,000, and stock futures are green, all at the same time Treasury is doubling bond buybacks to hold down yields on a debt load north of $40 trillion. 💰

  • The tension: CME's FedWatch tool shows markets now pricing real odds of a September rate hike, not a cut, so this rally is happening despite tighter policy expectations, not because of easier ones. ⚖️
  • What's next: watch whether the debasement trade holds through Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's Jackson Hole speech August 27-29 and Nvidia's August 26 earnings, both real tests of whether investors keep buying every asset at once or finally have to pick a lane. 🔍
