🚨 Geopolitics

US and Iran Hold Their Strike Pause for a Second Day, and Oil Craters on the Hope It Sticks

  • The tea: the US paused attacks on Iran for a second straight day Sunday and Tehran said it did too, a hiatus that began Friday after nearly two weeks of US strikes launched in retaliation for attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz. 🎯
  • The diplomacy angle: Iran says it will keep standing down as long as the US pause holds, following a China-led push through Pakistan to restart stalled talks, and President Trump has signaled he's open to reviving negotiations. 🕊️
  • The oil move: Brent crude slid to $90.28 a barrel, down about 8.2%, and WTI dropped to $83.37, down about 6.7%, as the market prices in a real chance the shooting stops. 🛢️
  • Why it matters: cheaper oil takes pressure off the same inflation math the Fed has to weigh at Wednesday's rate decision (Story 2), which is a big reason futures are ripping about 1% higher into the open. ⚖️

The Fed's Big Call Lands Wednesday, and This Time There's No Dot Plot to Hide Behind

Is This the Week the AI-Capex Story Gets Its Alibi, or Runs Out of Excuses?

  • The big idea: last week the market showed its hand, punishing Alphabet and Tesla even after real revenue growth, purely over how much they're spending on AI. This week, Microsoft, Meta, Apple and Amazon all face the identical test, with a Fed decision sitting right in the middle of it. 💰
  • The evidence: Alphabet's 2026 capex guidance now sits as high as $205 billion and Tesla just posted its first negative free cash flow in more than two years, the same week a $250 billion Nvidia-OpenAI backstop report broke, spending keeps escalating even as the market grows less patient with it. 🧮

  • TL;DR: this week is the cleanest test yet of whether Wall Street's patience with the AI buildout is genuinely running out, or whether last week was really about oil and Fed-hike fears piling on top of ordinary earnings jitters. 🔒

🔔 The Close

Wall Street Stabilizes as Apple's Rally Lifts the Dow and Oil's Reversal Cools the Week's Panic

The S&P 500 added about 0.05% to close near 7,412, the Nasdaq Composite slipped about 0.6% to near 24,988 as chip stocks lagged, the Dow Jones Industrial Average jumped 235 points (0.5%) to near 51,947, its best day this week, and the Russell 2000 slipped about 0.3%. 🟢

  • Why it matters: after a week where the AI-capex bill from yesterday's Big Brain dominated headlines, today was a reminder that oil and geopolitics can still swing this market harder than any single earnings report. ⚖️

🧠 Deep Thoughts

This Week Proved the Market Has Two Panic Buttons, and They Don't Always Agree

  • The takeaway: yesterday's Big Brain flagged the AI-capex bill; today showed that bill isn't the only thing moving this market. GOOGL and TSLA got punished Thursday on spending fears, but today's rally had nothing to do with AI discipline, it was oil and geopolitics deflating together that did the work. 💰
  • The evidence: Digital Realty jumped 13.5% today on a straightforward data-center beat with no capex hand-wringing attached, while Intel fell despite its best quarterly revenue growth in fifteen years and American Express fell despite beating on EPS, an even messier version of the growth-versus-spending split this Buzz keeps tracking. 🧮
  • The next 6-12 months: watch whether next week's Fed decision plus Microsoft, Meta, Apple and Amazon earnings, four Magnificent Seven prints and a rate call inside five days, forces the market to settle on one story (AI capex discipline) or keep swinging between two (AI capex and oil-and-rates). 🔍
