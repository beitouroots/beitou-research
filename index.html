🚨 Geopolitics

US and Iran Hold Their Strike Pause for a Second Day, and Oil Craters on the Hope It Sticks

  • The tea: the US paused attacks on Iran for a second straight day Sunday and Tehran said it did too, a hiatus that began Friday after nearly two weeks of US strikes launched in retaliation for attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz. 🎯
  • The diplomacy angle: Iran says it will keep standing down as long as the US pause holds, following a China-led push through Pakistan to restart stalled talks, and President Trump has signaled he's open to reviving negotiations. 🕊️
  • The oil move: Brent crude slid to $90.28 a barrel, down about 8.2%, and WTI dropped to $83.37, down about 6.7%, as the market prices in a real chance the shooting stops. 🛢️
  • Why it matters: cheaper oil takes pressure off the same inflation math the Fed has to weigh at Wednesday's rate decision (Story 2), which is a big reason futures are ripping about 1% higher into the open. ⚖️

Is This the Week the AI-Capex Story Gets Its Alibi, or Runs Out of Excuses?

💥 The Standout

Nvidia Weighs a $250 Billion Guarantee to Get OpenAI's Ohio Mega-Campus Built

  • The tea: the Wall Street Journal reported Nvidia is in talks to guarantee roughly $250 billion in financing so OpenAI can lease a planned 10-gigawatt data center campus in Pike County, Ohio, being developed by SoftBank's SB Energy on the former Portsmouth uranium enrichment site. 📰
  • The scale: the full project, including the Nvidia chips expected to power it, could eventually cost more than $500 billion; Nvidia is separately said to be discussing financing up to $350 billion of OpenAI's chip purchases, a second, distinct piece of the deal. 🧮
  • The status: nothing is finalized, first-phase capacity of about 800 megawatts isn't slated to come online until 2028, and the report traces to the Journal alone as of this writing, so treat the structure as a live negotiation, not a signed deal. 🔍
  • Why it matters: Nvidia stock fell 4.99% to $196.43 on the news, as traders debate whether backstopping OpenAI's buildout is a demand signal or a leverage red flag, exactly the growth-versus-spending question this week's Big Brain keeps circling back to. ⚖️

🧠 Deep Thoughts

Two Real Beats, Two Punished Stocks, and the Market Still Can't Decide What It's Afraid Of

  • The takeaway: this week's real theme isn't earnings quality, it's spending discipline. International Paper jumped on nothing but analyst optimism, Celestica just posted a genuine beat-and-raise on 62% revenue growth, and yet Nvidia and SanDisk both got hit today on fears about how much is being spent, or lent, to build out AI infrastructure. 💰
  • The evidence: Nvidia's stock fell nearly 5% on a report it might guarantee up to $250 billion just so OpenAI can lease one data center campus, the same day Chinese competitor CXMT's stock market debut reminded investors the AI-hardware moat isn't guaranteed either. 🧮
  • The next 6-12 months: watch whether Wednesday's Fed decision plus Microsoft, Meta, Apple and Amazon earnings settle the growth-versus-spending argument, or whether every fresh AI dollar committed, whether it's capex or a financing guarantee like Nvidia's, keeps getting read as a red flag regardless of the demand behind it. 🔍
