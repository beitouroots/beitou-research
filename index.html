💻 Technology / AI

AI Funding Jitters: OpenAI Reportedly Trims Its Ask, Western Digital Slips on a Beat

🌍 Geopolitics / Energy

Oil Stays Above $100 as Iran Headlines Keep Coming

  • The move: Brent is up 1.57% to $114.95 and WTI is up 1.81% to $101.27 in early pre-market quotes. Thursday's Brent settle was $113.17, a five-month high. 🛢️

France Is the New Bond Worry While the Fed Meeting Looms

🧠 The Big Brain · the 6-12 month bet
🧠 Deep Thoughts

The Cost of Capital Meets the AI Buildout

  • The big idea: this week's wobble ties together three things: AI spending that has to be financed, oil above $100, and long-term yields near multi-decade highs in the US and France. 🧐
  • The signal: a reported cut in OpenAI's target valuation and a Western Digital beat that still sold off suggest investors are getting pickier about who pays for AI capacity. 🏗️
  • The bull case: if yields settle and AI revenue keeps showing up, pullbacks like this one look like digestion, in the words of IG's Beauchamp. 🐂
  • The risk: if oil stays elevated and bond markets keep demanding more, financing costs stay high and the most capex-hungry names stay under pressure. 🐻
  • TL;DR: over the next 6 to 12 months, watch funding terms, long yields and oil as much as earnings beats. 🎯
