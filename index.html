🧠 The Big Brain · the 6-12 month bet
🧠 Deep Thoughts

AI's Next Financing Trick Is Equity-for-Compute, and It's Becoming the Rule, Not the Exception

  • The big idea: Akamai just handed Anthropic warrants for up to about 5% of its own stock as part of an $11.6 billion compute deal, the same equity-for-compute playbook already showing up in Oracle's roughly $300 billion OpenAI commitment and OpenAI's $11.9 billion CoreWeave deal. As 5%-plus yields make pure debt-funded data centers pricier, warrants and revenue-share stakes are becoming the cheaper way to lock in multi-year compute. 🏦
  • The read: tying a chunk of the payoff to future stock performance instead of upfront cash lowers near-term capex risk for suppliers, but it also deepens the circularity question investors keep raising about Nvidia, OpenAI, Oracle and CoreWeave essentially financing each other's backlogs. 🧮
  • The counter: cash-strapped AI labs get compute without blowing out the balance sheet, and infrastructure providers get real upside instead of a fixed-price contract that could go stale if yields keep climbing, supporters call it smart capital allocation, not a red flag. ⚖️
  • What's next: watch whether more of the roughly $1 trillion in announced AI infrastructure spending through 2027 shifts toward equity-linked deals as the 10-year hovers near 5.2%, and whether the circular financing web draws more scrutiny as it grows. 🔍
  • TL;DR: the AI capex story is not slowing down, it is just changing how it gets paid for, watch who is trading equity for compute over the next 6 to 12 months, because that is where the real leverage in this cycle is quietly building up. 🔒
