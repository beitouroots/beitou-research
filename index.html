Houthi Tanker Strikes Send Oil Ripping Toward $98 as the Iran War Hits a 12th Day

  • Why it matters: a 12th day of strikes with tanker attacks now spreading to Saudi shipping means the energy-driven inflation risk this Buzz keeps flagging just got sharper, right as the Fed refuses to rule out a hike (Story 3). ⚖️

💻 Tech Earnings

Alphabet and Tesla Both Beat on Revenue, Get Sold Anyway as AI Spending Fears Take Over

  • The tea: Alphabet posted Q2 revenue of $119.8 billion, up 24% year-over-year and above the $117 billion analysts expected, with Google Cloud revenue up 82% to $24.8 billion, its fastest growth in years. ☁️
  • The catch: Alphabet raised its full-year 2026 capex guidance to $195-205 billion (from $180-190 billion) and flagged another jump in 2027, sending Class A shares (GOOGL) down about 4.5% pre-market, while Class C (GOOG) traded around $330, down about 3.3%. 📉
  • Tesla's story: Tesla delivered a record 480,126 vehicles, up 25%, but posted its first negative free cash flow in more than two years (a $1.09 billion deficit) as capex surged 142% to $5.79 billion on AI, robotics and Optimus; TSLA fell about 5.7% pre-market to around $352. 🔋
  • Why it matters: two AI-spending beats, two stock drops, Wall Street just showed it will punish more capex even when the growth numbers are real, exactly the tension yesterday's Big Brain flagged (today's Big Brain). 🔍

The AI Capex Bill Just Landed, and the Market Doesn't Like What It Sees

  • The big idea: yesterday's Buzz flagged hyperscalers funding a $600 billion-plus AI infrastructure build partly with debt, betting the return shows up later, today Alphabet and Tesla gave the market its first real look at that bill and got sold anyway. 💰

Tesla delivered a record number of cars and still burned $1.09 billion in free cash flow, its first cash-flow deficit in two years, largely on AI and robotics spend. 🧮

  • TL;DR: the AI trade just passed its first real earnings test on revenue, and failed it on cash-flow discipline, that gap between growth and spending is now the single biggest swing factor for the rest of this earnings season. 🔒

GOOGL and TSLA got punished Thursday on spending fears, but today's rally had nothing to do with AI discipline, it was oil and geopolitics deflating together that did the work. 💰
