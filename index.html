  • The meeting: Trump said US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner held a three-hour "very good" and "very productive" sit-down with Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi in New York Tuesday, with another meeting planned soon, even as Trump kept up his public "annihilate" rhetoric toward Tehran at the UN. 🤝

  • Why it matters: this is the clearest sign yet that the sanctions-versus-diplomacy tug-of-war is finally tilting toward de-escalation, which is exactly why oil's giving back some of its war-premium gains even though it's still up nearly 40% year over year. 🔍

Boston Fed's Collins Backed Last Week's Hike, Now Warns Inflation Could Stay "Notably" Above Target

  • The tea: Boston Fed President Susan Collins said she supported last week's quarter-point hike, which took the fed funds rate to a 3.75% to 4% target range, the Fed's first hike since 2023. 🏛️

🧠 Deep Thoughts

The Fed Just Started Hiking Again for the First Time Since 2023, and Oil Is Still Up Nearly 40% Year Over Year

  • The big idea: last week the Fed raised rates a quarter point to 3.75% to 4%, its first hike since 2023, not a cut, while WTI crude is still up close to 40% year over year even after six straight down sessions. That's an unusual combination to see with the S&P near record highs. 🏦
  • The read: Boston Fed's Susan Collins is already flagging "notably" above-target inflation and expects one more hike before year-end, and she's explicitly pointing at geopolitics, meaning the Iran war's energy shock, as part of the reason. 🧮
  • The counter: chip and memory earnings are genuinely strong right now, Micron and its peers are seeing real demand, so the rally isn't pure multiple expansion, and a US-Iran and US-China thaw this week could pull both oil and rate anxiety lower fast. ⚖️
  • What's next: watch whether the Trump-Xi summit and Iran talks actually de-escalate energy prices enough to give the Fed room to stop hiking, because if oil snaps back on any new flare-up, a hiking Fed and a record-high, AI-led market get a lot harder to square. 🔍
  • TL;DR: the setup right now is a Fed hiking into elevated energy costs while stocks chase records on AI and chip strength, the next 6 to 12 months decide whether that's a soft landing with a hiccup or the start of a real stagflation scare. 🔒
