Hormuz Optimism Curdles Overnight: Trump Says the US Is Only "Semi-Negotiating" With Iran

  • The tea: President Trump told Axios over the weekend the US is only "semi-negotiating" with Iran, while Iranian officials denied any direct talks are underway, a sharp cooldown from last week's hopeful tone. 🚨

🏛️ The Fed

Under New Chair Kevin Warsh, the Fed Just Watched September Hike Odds Get Cut in Half

  • The tea: the FOMC voted 9 to 3 on July 29 to hold the fed funds rate at 3.50% to 3.75%, Chair Kevin Warsh's second meeting since taking over from Jerome Powell in May, with three members dissenting in favor of a hike. 🏛️
  • The whiplash: Friday's surprise July payroll drop knocked CME FedWatch's odds of a September 16 hike down to about 44%, from roughly two-thirds a week earlier, traders now see a hold as the more likely outcome. 🔄
  • The next tell: July's CPI lands Wednesday morning, the first major inflation read since that split decision, and it's the number most likely to move both the hawks and doves on the committee. 📅
  • Why it matters: a Fed this divided means every data point this week, starting with CPI, carries outsized weight for where rates go next. ⚖️

  • The big idea: Kevin Warsh took over the Fed in May promising tighter inflation discipline, and his committee has already split publicly, 9 to 3 in July, with three members wanting a hike he didn't deliver. That is an unusually visible fight for a still-new chair. 💰

  • The AI wrinkle: none of this is slowing the AI capex story, TSMC's July revenue grew 45% and this week's CoreWeave, Rocket Lab and Applied Materials prints will show whether that holds, so the next 6-12 months is really a fight between a divided Fed and a still-roaring AI cycle. 🔍
