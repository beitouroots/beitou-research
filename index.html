🏛️ Macro / The Fed

30-Year Yield Hits Its Highest Since 2002 as August PCE Inflation Lands This Morning

  • The tea: the 30-year Treasury yield topped 5.6% Tuesday, its highest since June 2002, while the 10-year closed at 5.28% after touching 5.29%, the highest since 2007. 📈
  • The data: August PCE, the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, drops at 8:30am ET along with the final Q2 GDP estimate, and economists expect core PCE to rise 0.3% month over month. 🧮
  • The mood: the consumer confidence index fell to 81.9 in September, its lowest in more than twelve years and well under the 89.2 forecast. 🔍
  • Why it matters: markets are already debating whether the Fed hikes again in October or waits until December, so a hot core print could tighten the screws on yields before the open. ⚠️

💻 Technology / AI

Anthropic's Leaked IPO Prospectus Targets a Valuation Above $2 Trillion, and a $42 Billion Loss Comes With It

  • The tea: per Reuters' review of the prospectus, Anthropic is preparing an IPO that could value it at more than $2 trillion, over double its estimated $965 billion valuation in May. 🚀
  • The numbers: 2025 revenue rose twelvefold to almost $4.6 billion, against a $42 billion net loss that includes an accounting charge of about $34 billion tied to financing instruments that could convert into shares. 💰
  • The spend: Anthropic put $7.33 billion into computing and infrastructure in 2025, roughly triple its 2024 outlay. 🔧
  • The timing: the IPO is expected after the US midterm elections in November, and the leak has raised fresh questions about the AI trade. ⏳
Sources Investing.com / Reuters Yahoo Finance Kiplinger

🌍 Geopolitics / Energy

Oil Slides as Middle East Exports Recover and the US Taps Its Emergency Reserve, Even With Iran Talks Stuck

  • The tea: WTI settled Tuesday at $89.38, down $3.22 or 3.5%, and Brent settled at $102.59, down $2.69 or 2.6%, as signs of recovering Middle East exports weighed on crude. 🛢️
  • The supply side: the US ordered another release from its emergency reserves, and Saudi Arabia has restored flows to around 3.5 million barrels a day through its East-West pipeline, an alternative route around Hormuz. 🚢
  • The catch: talks in New York produced limited progress and President Trump rejected Tehran's latest proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, so shipping through the strait is still well below pre-war levels. ⚠️
  • Why it matters: despite the daily drop, oil is still headed for a monthly gain, and energy is a big piece of the inflation story the Fed is watching. 🔍

🧠 Deep Thoughts

The Long Bond Is Now the Gatekeeper for the AI Mega-IPO Era

  • The big idea: the 30-year Treasury yield is above 5.6%, the highest since 2002, just as Anthropic prepares an IPO targeting more than $2 trillion. When the risk-free long bond pays that much, every giant growth listing has to clear a much higher hurdle. 🏦
  • The read: Anthropic reported almost $4.6 billion of 2025 revenue against a $42 billion net loss, and it plans to list after the November midterms. Investors will be pricing years of future cash flows at a discount rate that keeps climbing. 🧮
  • The counter: demand for AI hardware is showing up in the numbers, Micron guided to roughly $50 billion of quarterly revenue, so bulls will argue growth can outrun the discount rate. ⚖️
  • What's next: watch core PCE today, the Fed's October and December decisions, and whether the long end calms down before the IPO window opens after the midterms. 🔍
  • TL;DR: over the next 6 to 12 months, the price of long-term money may matter as much as the AI story itself, so track the 30-year alongside the earnings. 🔒
