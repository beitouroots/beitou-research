🏛️ The Fed

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh Takes the Jackson Hole Podium for the First Time, and the Bond Market Isn't Waiting Patiently

  • The tea: Warsh delivers his first Jackson Hole keynote as Fed Chair at 10 AM ET today, the marquee event of the week for markets. 🎤

💳 Fintech / M&A

The Year's Biggest Fintech Buyout Just Died, and PayPal Is Cratering Because of It

  • The tea: Advent International and Stripe have abandoned their pursuit of PayPal, walking away from a bid of $60.50 a share that valued the company at more than $53 billion. 💔
  • The reaction: PayPal shares fell as much as 16% pre-market Friday, wiping out the takeover premium that had built up since the offer went public in July. 📉
  • The why: PayPal's board reportedly saw the bid as inadequate, while the buyers faced their own regulatory and financing hurdles on what would have ranked among the largest leveraged buyouts ever attempted. ⚖️
  • What's next: Bloomberg notes a future approach from the Advent-Stripe group "remains possible" if conditions shift, but for now PayPal is back to being a standalone story. 🔍

🧠 Deep Thoughts

Nvidia Says the AI Buildout Still Has Room to Run, Marvell's Stock Reaction Says the Market Is Getting Pickier About Believing It

  • The big idea: hyperscaler AI capex is tracking toward roughly $780 to $844 billion in 2026 alone and could cross $1.1 trillion in 2027, more than double the entire 2022-through-2024 total, according to JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs estimates. 💰
  • The read: Nvidia's own 70% revenue growth guide for fiscal 2028 says the company driving this whole trade doesn't see a slowdown coming, that's about as bullish a data point as this cycle gets. 🧮
  • The tension: Marvell got punished this morning for the exact same broad AI-demand story, just because its forward detail was thinner, a sign the market is shifting from rewarding growth spending to demanding proof it traces to real demand. ⚖️
  • What's next: Goldman pegs US data-center power demand jumping from 41 gigawatts in 2026 to 66 gigawatts in 2027, watch whether power and grid constraints, not enterprise adoption, become the real limit on this trade over the next 6 to 12 months. 🔌
  • TL;DR: the AI capex story isn't cracking, but investors just started grading it on specificity instead of vibes, and that shift is worth watching all the way into next year's guidance. 🔒
