💶 Europe / Markets

The Euro Hits a 17-Month Low as French Budget Gridlock Blows Out Bond Spreads

  • The tea: the euro fell as much as 0.8% to $1.1161 against the dollar in Asian trading, its lowest since May 2025. 💶
  • The spread: the gap between French and German 10-year yields moved above 150 basis points, the widest since the height of the eurozone debt crisis. 📈
  • The cause: investors worry about France's deficit and whether budget-fixing plans survive a divided parliament, with a presidential election due in April 2027. 🗳️
  • Why it matters: contagion fears are spreading across the region while US yields stay elevated, a combo that keeps the dollar firm and rates in focus into the US open. 🔍
