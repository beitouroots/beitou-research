🏛️ The Fed / Inflation

September CPI Runs Hot, and an October Rate Hike Is Suddenly Back on the Table

  • The print: headline CPI rose 0.6% in September and 3.7% from a year ago, vs. 3.6% expected (August was 3.4%). Core CPI rose 0.3% on the month and 2.6% on the year, vs. 0.2% and 2.5% expected. 🔥
  • The driver: gasoline did the heavy lifting, but airfares, used vehicles, recreation and medical care all chipped in. Edward Jones' James McCann flags core running 3.4% annualized over the past three months. ⛽
  • The odds: traders sharply raised bets on a hike at the Oct. 27-28 meeting, from roughly 1 in 5 before the data to roughly a coin flip, per Reuters and CME-based trackers. The policy range is 3.75% to 4%. 🎲
  • Fed speak: before the print, New York Fed President John Williams said one more hike this year looks appropriate but none should be needed in 2027, per Bloomberg. 🎤
Sources Reuters CNBC CoinDesk
02
📈 Macro / Markets

Futures Flip Red, Bonds Get Smoked, and the Dollar Hits an 18-Month High

  • The flip: futures were green before 8:30 ET, then rolled over. S&P 500 futures are down 0.39% and Nasdaq 100 futures down 0.74% pre-market. 🔄
  • Bond check: the 10-year yield jumped about 9 basis points to 5.30%, and the 2-year rose 13 basis points to 4.92%, per Reuters. The short end is screaming hike. 📈
  • Everything else: the dollar index rose 0.55% to 102.75, an 18-month high, and gold fell 1.6% to $4,088. Bitcoin dropped about $1,000 to near $82,800, per CoinDesk. 💵
  • Sector tell: tech was the weakest S&P sector pre-market and energy the strongest. Rates up plus oil up is a tough combo for growth stocks. ⚖️
Sources Reuters CoinDesk Rallies

💻 Technology / AI

ASML Says AI Demand Will Outrun Its Supply Through 2028, but Chips Still Can't Catch a Bid

  • $ASML's print: record Q3 net sales of €11.7 billion, a 57.5% gross margin and €5.0 billion of net income, per its release. Q4 guide is €14.2 billion to €15.2 billion. 🔬
  • The quote that matters: ASML now expects planned supply for 2027 and 2028 will not fully meet customer demand, and some customers want to place orders for 2029 and even 2030. Full-year 2026 sales guide: €44 billion to €45 billion. 🚀
  • The catch: the chip sector is still bleeding. The PHLX Semiconductor Index fell five straight days, the semis ETF dropped about 2% after CPI, and Micron ($MU) and SanDisk are lower pre-market. 📉
  • Up next: Taiwan Semiconductor ($TSM) reports Thursday. If TSMC echoes ASML, the AI bulls get their rebuttal. 🗓️

🧠 The Big Brain · the 6-12 month bet
🧠 Deep Thoughts

Bonds Are Getting Interesting Again: When the 10-Year Pays 5.3%, Cash Flow Beats Hope

  • The big idea: energy is leaking into everything. Core CPI is creeping up, the Fed is in a hiking cycle, and Mercer Advisors' Donald Calcagni says markets are pricing one more hike this year and two next year. 🧮
  • The bond case: with the 10-year near 5.3% and the 2-year near 4.9%, high-quality bonds pay real income again and, as Calcagni puts it, regain their power to diversify a stock portfolio. 🏦
  • The stock case: AI capex is not dying. ASML says 2027 and 2028 demand will exceed its planned supply. Companies with real earnings, like BlackRock today, can still win in a 5% world. 🐂
  • The risk: long-duration growth stocks get repriced every time a CPI or oil headline surprises. Brent above $100 plus a hawkish Fed is the bear's favorite recipe. 🐻
  • TL;DR: over the next 6 to 12 months, balance beats bravado. Own profitable growth, let bonds pay you to wait, and keep some dry powder for the next rate-scare dip. 🎯
