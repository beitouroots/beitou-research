US Navy Fires on a Tanker Breaking Its Iran Blockade, Brent Pushes Toward $90

  • The tea: a US military helicopter fired on the rudder of the Panama-flagged containership Vela Nova early Tuesday after it ignored warnings and tried to breach Washington's blockade of Iranian ports in the Gulf of Oman, sparking a fire that was put out. All 17 crew were accounted for. 🚨

  • Why it matters: every extra dollar of Hormuz risk premium flows straight into this morning's 8:30am CPI print, exactly the number Chair Warsh needs clean before September (today's Big Brain). ⚖️

🏛️ The Fed

CPI Lands at 8:30am ET, the Number That Decides Chair Warsh's September Move

  • The tea: the Bureau of Labor Statistics releases July CPI this morning at 8:30am ET. Economists expect headline inflation up 0.1% month over month and easing to 3.4% year over year, down from June's 3.5%. 📅
  • The stakes: traders are pricing roughly a coin-flip, about 50-50, chance of a September rate hike. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh delivered a hawkish surprise at his debut FOMC in July, with nine of eighteen participants penciling in a 2026 hike and the statement dropping its easing bias. 🔄
  • The Warsh read: per FT reporting, he remains open to a September hike if inflation runs hot over the coming weeks; in July congressional testimony he shot down any "mission accomplished" read on inflation. 🧊
  • Why it matters: a hot print reopens the door to a hike, a soft one locks in a hold, this is the single number most likely to move markets before the open (today's Big Brain has the full setup). ⚖️

🧠 The Big Brain · the 6-12 month bet
🧠 Deep Thoughts

Tech's Earnings Are Still Great. The Premium You Pay for Them Just Got Cut by Two Thirds

  • The big idea: a year ago, investors were paying roughly 35% more for a dollar of expected tech earnings than for a dollar of S&P 500 earnings. Today that premium has compressed to about 10%, even as CoreWeave and Super Micro just posted some of the loudest AI-demand beats of the cycle. 💰
  • The bull case: multiples cooling while fundamentals stay hot is a healthier setup than a market where price and earnings both have to be perfect, it gives beats like CoreWeave's and Super Micro's room to actually move stocks instead of being priced in a year ahead of time. 📶
  • The bear case: valuation compression can also just mean the easy multiple-expansion leg of the AI trade is over, and from here every quarter has to deliver on its own, with a new hawkish Fed chair and a live Strait of Hormuz standoff both able to yank the discount rate around at the same time. 🧮
  • The wrinkle: this CPI print and Warsh's September decision matter more to that multiple than almost any single earnings call, a hawkish surprise raises the discount rate on every future AI dollar at once, a soft print does the opposite. 🔍
  • TL;DR: watch the next two quarters for whether AI infrastructure names keep clearing a lower valuation bar with real numbers, or whether the bar dropped because the growth story itself is cooling, that's the trade behind today's calendar, not just today's CPI headline. 🔒
