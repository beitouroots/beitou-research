📅 Earnings / Markets

Delta Cuts Its Full-Year Profit Outlook on Fuel Costs

  • The tea: Delta ($DAL) reported September-quarter adjusted EPS of $1.72 versus $1.70 a year earlier, on adjusted revenue of $17.6 billion, up 16%, per its 8-K. 📅
  • The cut: full-year EPS outlook is now $5.10 to $5.60 with free cash flow of about $2.5 billion, down from the $6.50 to $7.50 range it held before. ✂️
  • The Q4 view: the company expects December-quarter EPS of $1.15 to $1.65 on fuel of about $4.25 a gallon, with revenue up about 20%. ⛽
  • The scorecard: the EPS figure came in below consensus, which sources put between $1.82 and $1.89. 🔍
