🚀 AI Software

Palantir Just Delivered the Biggest Earnings Beat of the Season, and Karp Calls It "Otherworldly"

  • The tea: Palantir posted Q2 revenue of $1.935 billion, up 93% year over year, with U.S. commercial revenue surging 149% to $764 million, per the company's SEC filing. 🚀
  • The raise: Palantir hiked full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $8.15-$8.16 billion (82% growth) and U.S. commercial guidance to above $3.42 billion (134%+ growth), its third straight quarter of raising the outlook. 📈

  • Why it matters: this is the clearest proof yet that AI software demand is converting into real, recognized revenue and not just capex promises, right as the market debates whether the whole AI trade is overbuilt (today's Big Brain). ⚖️

September Rate-Hike Odds Jump to 59% as Today's JOLTS Sets Up Friday's Jobs Report
